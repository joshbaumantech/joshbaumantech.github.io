Services

What we handle, so you don't

Engagements are scoped to what your business actually runs on — not a bundled package with fourteen line items you'll never use.

Everyday IT support

The workstations, printers, phones, Wi-Fi, and point-of-sale that have to work before anything else does. Reached directly, fixed quickly, explained in plain language.

Cybersecurity & risk

Practical protection sized to a small business: multi-factor authentication, backups that have actually been tested, phishing defense, and a written incident plan for the day something goes wrong.

Networks & infrastructure

Firewalls, switching, wireless coverage, cabling, and cameras — designed once, correctly, so the next five years are quiet ones.

Cloud & productivity

Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace: migrations, licensing that isn't overspent, shared drives with sane permissions, and email that lands in inboxes instead of spam folders.

Compliance & data privacy

HIPAA, PCI, student data privacy, cyber-insurance questionnaires, and vendor security reviews — translated out of the acronyms and into a short list of things to do.

Fractional IT leadership

A seat at the table when you're choosing a vendor, planning a build-out, budgeting for next year, or deciding whether that quote is fair. Strategy without a full-time salary.

Approach

How an engagement starts

Three conversations, usually inside two weeks. You'll know what you're buying before you buy it.

  1. 01

    A conversation

    Thirty minutes, no charge, no pitch deck. What's breaking, what's slow, what keeps you up at night, and what you're already paying for.

  2. 02

    An honest assessment

    A walk-through of your systems and a short written summary: what's healthy, what's exposed, what's costing you money. It's yours to keep either way.

  3. 03

    A plan you approve

    Fixed scope, fixed price, in priority order — urgent risks first, nice-to-haves last. Ongoing support is monthly and cancellable. No multi-year lock-in.

The principal

Josh Bauman

Nearly 30 years in information technology — most of it spent in environments where downtime isn't an inconvenience and a data breach isn't hypothetical.

Josh has built and run technology operations across healthcare, higher education, and K–12 public education, where the demands are enterprise-grade but the budgets rarely are. That's the discipline he brings to small business: figuring out what genuinely has to be done, doing it well, and skipping the rest.

He serves on state and national cybersecurity bodies, including the Missouri State Cybersecurity Committee and the MS-ISAC Executive Committee, where he also acts as a state lead for the K–12 working group. He's part of the leadership team for the Missouri Student Data Privacy Alliance, co-hosts the K12 Tech Talk podcast, and was named to EdTech Magazine's 2025 list of 30 K–12 IT influencers to follow.

He lives and works in Festus, and serves businesses throughout Jefferson County and the greater St. Louis area — in person when it matters, remotely when it doesn't.
